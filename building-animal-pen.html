WhereBuildings window (the house button), placed within 40 blocks

Animal pen: A fenced yard with a gate and a trough chest. Put up to 8 tamed farm animals in it; each day they produce wool, meat and more, and pairs have young.

Where soldiers stand

This building has no soldier posts.

How it looks as your village grows

Buildings you build follow your village's size, like the castle:

Village sizeBuilt of
HamletWood: planks, timber beams, thatch roofs
TownStone: castle stone, stone brick beams, tiled roofs
CityIron: riveted iron plates, iron beams, iron roofs

Animals

Up to 8 tamed farm animals live in a pen; each day they produce wool, milk, eggs and more into its trough chest. Birds go in a bird cage instead.
