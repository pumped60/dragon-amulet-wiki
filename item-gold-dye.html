Gold dye is a crafting material. A little bottle of gold dye. Used for stained glass, carpets, flags, tapestries, and to dye a bed's blanket (hold it and tap the bed).

How to get it

  • Craft: Honey → Gold dye ×2 at Workbench
  • Craft: Gold bar → Gold dye ×6 at Furnace
  • Buy from the Herbalist: 18 coins
