Getting started

Dragon Amulet is a side-view adventure of mining, crafting, building and war. Rebuild your village, raise an army, beat the bosses in order and slay Vorthax the dragon to claim the Dragon Amulet.

Your first day

  • You begin alone in the wild with a weapon, a wooden pickaxe and a wooden axe.
  • Find the abandoned village on your island and talk to the Quartermaster waiting in the overgrown castle. He rebuilds it as your home in about a day.
  • While he works, chop trees, mine stone, coal and iron, craft tools at a workbench and smelt bars at a furnace.
  • Sleep in a bed at night to skip to morning. The last bed you slept in is your spawn point: the place you come back to if you fall.
  • When the village is rebuilt, wanderers arrive. Talk to each one and choose a job: a shopkeeper, a woodcutter, farmer or miner, or a soldier.
  • Grow your army, then take on the bosses in order, starting with the Goblin Beastmaster.

The Tutorial on the title screen walks you through all of this in a small world of its own.

Saving

The game saves by itself every 30 seconds, and again when you choose Save and quit. Each world has its own slot, and Continue on the title screen lists them.

Saves are kept on the device, by the browser or app you play in. The claude.ai game page, the play website and the computer and phone apps each keep their own worlds, so a world made in one does not show up in another. On the claude.ai game page, World 1 is also saved to your account.

Playing with friends

One player chooses Multiplayer, then Host, and shares the room code. Friends choose Multiplayer, type the code and press Join. Everyone plays in the host's world, and the host's world is the one that is saved.

Everyone has to be on the same copy of the game: a game hosted on the claude.ai link and one hosted on the play website or the app cannot see each other, and on claude.ai it must be the very same link. Join needs someone hosting right then; to get back into a world you hosted, use Host a saved world.
