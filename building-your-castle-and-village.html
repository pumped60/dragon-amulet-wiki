Your castle and village

Your castle, the village houses, the woodcutter's lodge and the farmhouse are rebuilt as the village grows.

Materials by village size

Village sizeBuilt of
HamletWood: planks, timber beams, thatch roofs
TownStone: castle stone, stone brick beams, tiled roofs
CityIron: riveted iron plates, iron beams, iron roofs

Shop houses

Each shopkeeper's house follows its own shop level instead: level 1 wood, level 2 stone, level 3 iron. Level 2 adds a third floor with a bed; level 3 adds a balcony, gold trim, a roof banner, paintings and rugs.

Fire and toughness

Wood and thatch can burn in raids and storms; stone and iron cannot. Iron plate needs an iron pickaxe or better to break.

Guards in the castle

The castle's towers have arrow slits two blocks high where soldiers stand, so they can shoot out but nobody can walk in. The upper hall has the portal, a throne and beds for villagers who take shelter.
