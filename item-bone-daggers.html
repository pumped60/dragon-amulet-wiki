Bone daggers is a weapon (tier 1 of 8 in its upgrade line).

How to get it

  • Craft: Bone ×4 + Oak planks ×2 + Sticks ×2 → Bone daggers at Workbench
  • Buy from the Blacksmith: 5 coins
