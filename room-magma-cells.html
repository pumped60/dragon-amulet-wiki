Cell block (magma keep): Barred cells along the wall with riveted lintels and shackles, square piers and a low iron ceiling. Deep stone floor; walls of cell stone.

Where it is

Halfway down the magma realm, in its middle: a maze of eighteen rooms on three levels beside the throne room. The Magma Knight rules it. A tunnel leads to its door from the portal you arrive by. You find this room on these levels: Upper hall, Hall, Dungeon cell.

Who you meet there

  • Magma macebearer × 2
  • Magma archer × 2

Counted in a freshly made world. Guards sleep until you first see their room, then they all wake.
