Amethyst is a crafting material. A purple gem from amethyst crystals in caves. Used in the cloud charm, the sun amulet, the mana rune and the silver staff.

How to get it

  • Buy from the Jeweler: 30 coins
  • Mined from Amethyst crystal

Used to make
