Sand is a block you can place. Loose sand. It falls when nothing holds it up; smelt it into glass.

How to get it

  • Dropped by the Dune scorpion: 1–3 (50% chance)
  • Dropped by the Sand wraith: 1–2 (60% chance)
  • Dropped by the scarab: 1–2 (40% chance)
