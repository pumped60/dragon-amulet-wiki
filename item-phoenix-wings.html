Phoenix wings is an accessory you wear once you have it. Wings, pair 9 of 10. Hold W (on a phone, the Wings button) to fly, steer left and right, hold down to dive. 60 seconds of flight; they recharge on the ground. You climb and fly 89% faster than with the first pair. Dropped by the Magma Knight.

How to get it

Found, mined or dropped in the world.
