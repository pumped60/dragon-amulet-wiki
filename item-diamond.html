Diamond

Diamond
TypeMaterial
Sells for40 coins

Diamond is a crafting material.

How to get it

  • Dropped by Grukk the Ogre King: 2–4 (100% chance)
  • Mined from Diamond ore (with a Gold pickaxe or better), found deep underground, in small veins of 3 or 4 in stone and deep stone from a little under halfway down toward the magma realm (about 43%) to the black stone above it. Dig straight down from the surface and light your way
  • Gold chests
