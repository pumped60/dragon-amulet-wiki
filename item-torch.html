Torch

Torch
TypeBlock
Sells for—
Hardness0.05
Light8

Torch is a block you can place. A small flame for a wall or floor. Keeps the dark and some monsters away.

How to get it

  • Craft: Sticks + Charcoal → Torch ×4 at By hand
  • Buy from the Shipwright: 15 coins for 10
  • Mined from Torch on a fence
