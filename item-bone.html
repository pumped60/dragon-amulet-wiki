Bone is a crafting material. Dropped by skeletons and frost monsters. Makes bone daggers and black powder for bombs.

How to get it

  • Dropped by the Skeleton archer: 1–2 (45% chance)
  • Dropped by the Mossy skeleton: 1–2 (50% chance)
  • Dropped by the Frost archer: 1–2 (40% chance)
  • Dropped by the Frost brute: 1–2 (50% chance)
  • Wolfs kept in a pen give it each morning
