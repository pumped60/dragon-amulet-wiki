Drake scale is a crafting material. A hard, warm scale from a drake of the obsidian caves.

How to get it

  • Dropped by the ember_drake: 1–2 (60% chance)
  • Dropped by the ash_wyvern: 1–2 (50% chance)
  • Dropped by the obsidian_wyrmling: 1 (35% chance)
