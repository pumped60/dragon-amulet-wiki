Blue dye

Blue dye
TypeMaterial
Sells for1 coins

Blue dye is a crafting material. A little bottle of blue dye. Used for stained glass, carpets, flags, tapestries, and to dye a bed's blanket (hold it and tap the bed).

How to get it

  • Craft: Blue flower → Blue dye ×2 at Cauldron
  • Craft: Sapphire → Blue dye ×4 at Cauldron
  • Buy from the Herbalist: 6 coins
  • Buy from the Tailor: 6 coins
