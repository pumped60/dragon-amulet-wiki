Oak planks

Oak planks
TypeBlock
UseSawn oak for floors, walls and furniture
Sells for1 coins
Hardness0.5

Oak planks is a block you can place. Sawn oak for floors, walls and furniture.

How to get it

  • Craft: Oak log → Oak planks ×4 at By hand
  • Buy from the Shipwright: 5 coins for 20
  • Mined from Broken plank
