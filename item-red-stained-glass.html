Red stained glass is a block you can place. Red stained glass you walk past, like a back wall: it stands behind you and anyone else. For a solid wall of it, make the red stained glass block.

How to get it

  • Craft: Glass ×2 + Red brick → Red stained glass ×2 at Furnace
  • Craft: Sand ×2 + Red dye → Red stained glass at Furnace
