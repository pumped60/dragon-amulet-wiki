Grukk the Ogre King

Grukk the Ogre King
Order6 of 8
Health13,000
Damage62 per hammer
Soldiers needed10
WhereDeepest chamber of the orc cave base

Grukk the Ogre King is boss 6 on the ladder.

How it fights

Huge reach and knockback; enraged he calls ogres and slams the ground

Reward

Gold bars, rubies, diamonds, a treasure chest, his war maul

Bring an army

With fewer soldiers or fighting pets near it than it needs, only a small part of your damage gets through (15% alone) and it heals as the fight goes on. Every boss enrages at half health.
