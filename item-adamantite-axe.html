Adamantite axe
TypeTool
Tier8 of 8

Sells for880 coins

Adamantite axe is a tool (tier 8 of 8 in its upgrade line). Chops trees, bamboo, cacti and mushroom trees.

How to get it

  • Craft: Adamantite bar ×16 + Oak planks ×3 + Sticks ×2 → Adamantite axe at Workbench
  • Buy from the Blacksmith: 4401 coins

Upgrade line

Owning a better tier replaces the one before it.
