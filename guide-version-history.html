Version history

Every update, newest first. Each update adds one to the last number of the version (0.9.1, 0.9.2 …).

Beta 0.9.159

  • Start screens on phones and computers: checked on two computer screens, two phones held sideways, a phone held upright and a tablet. Every window now shows one way to close it (the X; the small Back or Close beside it is gone, the X does the same thing). On phones held sideways the title buttons are bigger with larger text, and windows no longer run under the corner X. Hints and descriptions on the start screens are easier to read. On the Multiplayer screen the room code box fills the row beside a normal-sized Join, and Copy bug report is a small button at the bottom. The wiki link is off the title screen.

Beta 0.9.158

  • Doors always whole: ruined villages (the tutorial too) had doors with crumbled stone, moss, castle stone or air in one half. Every door is now both halves, open or closed together; a lone top half becomes a whole door.
  • Every big piece checked: a chest whose second cell was a door slides one or two blocks over (its contents and the village jobs that use it go along); doors, workbenches, furnaces, chests and cupboards never sit on a slope (the ground under them and their own cells are levelled, also when you place one).

Beta 0.9.157

  • Stations always full size: a workbench is always 2 blocks wide, a furnace 2 by 2, a chest 2 wide and a cupboard 2 tall, in every wood (spruce, birch and the other wood workbenches and cupboards were one block before). Whatever sits in that space gives way: grass, plants, rugs and rubble are cleared, natural rock is cleared, and a block someone built comes out as an item to pick up. Doors, chests, other stations and unbreakable blocks are never replaced. Placing one still needs room where solid blocks are, but plants no longer stop it. Stations never move one cell sideways to fit any more.

Beta 0.9.156

  • Tutorial rebuild fixed: the Quartermaster never finished rebuilding the tutorial village (the tutorial switches off monsters and world events, and the rebuild was switched off with them). It now takes about a minute, and the Recruiter, his room and the wanderers come when it is done.
  • Tutorial order: while the village is rebuilt you do Elder Rowan's errand: the cave, 8 stone, the coal, and the 5 stone back to her. Then the rebuild step shows how far the repairs are, and the village steps (miners, Recruiter, chest, building) start once it is done.
  • Tutorial card: it scrolls with a finger on phones, shows "more" when part of the task is hidden, and a tap on the text opens the whole task.
  • Bigger X buttons: the X at the top right on phones is bigger, the window X on computers is bigger, and a tap just beside any X still closes the window.
  • Windows in their own look: the crafting window at a workbench is that workbench's wood (the furnace stays stone); a chest window is planks of the chest's wood with iron corners; a barrel has iron hoops, a cupboard or desk a framed panel, a shelf plain boards; a gold chest is gold and a sky chest is cloud blue. The name line shows what you opened, like Spruce barrel.
  • Chests in every wood: Spruce, Birch, Palm, Jungle and Dark oak chests, made at the workbench like the oak chest with that wood's planks. They hold the same and give themselves back when broken.
  • Game status page: https://pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play/status/ lists outages, crashes and known problems with what was done about them. The title screen links to it.

Beta 0.9.155

  • Black screen on phones fixed: the Claude app on a phone does not turn sideways, so the card asking you to turn the phone stayed up for good and its small text was hard to see. The card now has a big Play upright button (Settings > Controls > Upright phone changes it back) and larger text.

Beta 0.9.154

  • Tutorial fixes: the workbenches (2 blocks), furnaces (4 blocks) and chests in the ruined village are full size from the start (they only grew after the rebuild), and dug ground shows a dirt or stone back wall like a real world instead of open sky.

Beta 0.9.153

  • Tutorial errand moved: you talk to Elder Rowan while the Quartermaster rebuilds the village; she asks for 5 stone from the little cave, so the cave step mines 8 stone (5 for her, 3 to build with) and you take her the 5 right after the coal, before building.
  • Tutorial screen on small phones: every class sits in one row on short screens, so nothing hides below the edge; it still scrolls (with a visible bar) if anything does not fit.
  • How often wanderers come: after you claim your village, each morning has a 1 in 5 chance of a wanderer, and one always comes if 5 days pass without one. Rescued captives also come to the village, and the first rebuild brings two.

Beta 0.9.152

  • Phones play sideways: on a phone or tablet held upright, a card asks you to turn it sideways and the game waits (a shared world keeps running for the others). Tapping the card also tries full screen with the sideways lock on phones that allow it. Settings > Controls > Upright phone > Allow upright turns the card off. The social media demo is made for an upright phone and never shows it.

Beta 0.9.151

  • Pickaxe tiers shown: a block that needs a stronger pickaxe says which tier, in three places: a label over the block while you aim a weaker pickaxe at it, a message when you swing at it (what it needs and what you hold), and its item text. Every pickaxe text starts with its tier and the blocks it opens up. Tiers: 1 wooden, copper, silver; 2 iron; 3 gold; 4 diamond; 5 storm; 6 mythril; 7 adamantite.

  • Tutorial: no stone ends (the sea is the edge) and no big rock; the jump ledge has grass on top only; the castle is rebuilt in a tenth of a real world's time; both wanderers become miners (the only job offered there); your first soldier is hired from the Recruiter; stone and coal come from the cave; new steps craft a hoe, till three blocks and plant carrot seeds; the sheep and rabbit roam the meadow, and once tamed you lead one into a pen of eight fence posts; the class screen scrolls on phones.

Pick a class; saved in slot 7.

  • Buildings take two taps: the first tap shows a see-through picture of the building where it would go (green when it fits, red when something is in the way); a second tap on it builds it; a quick double tap never builds; tap elsewhere to move it; the picture goes when the mouse leaves it, when you walk away, or with Esc or Cancel.
  • Boulders fly through animals: a trebuchet boulder or cannonball kills the animals in its path and lands where you aimed, instead of stopping on the first hawk that crossed its arc.

Beta 0.9.144

  • Scene editor zoom: pinch on a phone or press − and + to zoom out far enough to see a whole scene.

Beta 0.9.137

  • Battle plans for the big bosses: walking up to Vorthax's lair, the Sky Ranger's throne hall or the Magma Knight's throne room with soldiers asks how to attack. Ambush opens the posts window over the room: put soldiers on spots spread across its floor and ledges, so one breath or sweep cannot catch them all. When the boss wakes they spring the trap: Vorthax is forced down onto the floor for 5 seconds, where swords reach it, and the Sky Ranger or Magma Knight is stunned for 5 seconds (stars circle its head); every ambusher attacks at once. Waves sends 3, 5, 8 or 10 at a time; the rest wait outside the room and the next wave charges in when the one inside is down to one fighter (everyone goes when Vorthax comes out after you). Charge takes everyone in together. Soldiers waiting or hiding still count as the army you brought.
  • Soldiers keep up: the soldiers in your party move at your own speed, sprint and flight included, and one who has fallen more than 6 blocks behind runs a little faster until it catches up.
  • Report a bug stays in the game: Send posts the report from inside the game and thanks you; nothing opens in another tab. A report that cannot be sent yet (no connection) is kept and sent by itself later.
  • An X on every menu window on computers, in its top right corner (the God menu, Army, Buildings, Map, crafting and the rest). Phones keep the big X in the corner of the screen.
  • Vorthax has a new, more detailed look in the Scaleborn's red (a shade softer): bat wings with bones that beat as it flies, horns, a glowing eye, belly scales, spikes down its back, a swaying tail and clawed legs.
  • The crafting window shows where you are: its title says Crafting, Workbench, Furnace or Workbench and Furnace. At a workbench the window is warm brown wood, at a furnace grey stone, and by hand or with both stations near it stays purple.
  • A Stack button sits under Near and Hide whenever a chest you have opened is within 15 blocks: it puts the items in your bag into the nearby chests that already hold them. The crafting window no longer has its own Stack button. Long chest names fit on the chest window's title line.
  • Hooks come loose: when the block your grappling hook holds is broken (by you, a bomb or a friend), the hook falls and you drop.
  • Farmers gather what you have found: the Head farmer only offers berries, herbs, honey and the rest once you have found some yourself, and farmers only cut wild crops you have found (the farmhouse field is always theirs). Every crop grows wild somewhere in the world, at least one patch of each.
  • Woodcutters replant: every tree they fell gets a sapling where it stood, and every sapling grows into a tree two mornings after it was planted, near you or not. Woodcutters bring half as many logs, and pets that gather bring half as much.
  • Craft from your checklist: any recipe in the crafting window has a Checklist button beside Pin. The item goes on your side list with what it needs and where it is made. Tap it there: it tells you what is still missing or which station to stand by, and when you have everything and stand by that station (a longship or warship at the workbench, bars at the furnace) it crafts it and leaves the list.
  • Castle posts: the castle has a soldier post beside every arrow slit in its two corner towers (eight in all), like a watchtower. Press F on a tower floor and pick Place soldiers here, or use the Castle row in Buildings. Posted soldiers come back when the world loads.
  • Shooting through arrow slits works both ways: an enemy outside can shoot at a soldier (or you) standing at a slit, but about half of those shots hit the stone around the window and stop.
  • More torches: the castle and every building of your village get wall torches on an even grid, about five blocks apart and four rows up, on every floor and up the towers, wherever a room is not already lit.
  • Villagers and their job blocks: a villager starts selling or working again as soon as their job block (the smith's anvil, the cook's pot and the rest) is within 15 blocks, and keeps using that block however far they go until it is broken. Several villagers can share one. Moving a villager into another home brings one of their job blocks along.
  • Workers wait for their foreman: miners, woodcutters and farmers only set out again while the head miner, forester or head farmer is at the workplace (whatever they carry still comes home).
  • Wanderers: one arrives on about one morning in five, and never more than five days apart. Shop houses always match the castle's stage, so a new one is stone or iron when the castle is.

Steam delivers its updates, worlds can follow you between computers through the Steam Cloud, the Steam overlay works, and it earns thirteen achievements: the village claimed, a town, a city, a first tamed animal, a conquered faction and each of the eight bosses. Worlds that already beat a boss earn it when loaded; the test world never does. The browser game and the free Mac and Windows apps are unchanged.

Beta 0.9.135

They now stand on the steps. Any enemy that would start inside a block (an orc in its hut wall, the soldiers a boss calls) steps out to the nearest open spot. Older worlds are fixed when they load.

Beta 0.9.134

Only the gap beside the new building is levelled now; the others keep their ground.
  • Fishing village: in a new world the fishing village no longer shares its shore with the goblins' wooden lookout tower. The village was placed before the goblin island's lookouts were built, so on some worlds a lookout went up inside it.

Beta 0.9.133

  • Miners' chest: when the game nudged the miners' hut chest one block left to fit it, the miners kept delivering to the empty spot and their ore was lost. The hut (and the lodge and farmhouse) now follow the chest, and older worlds find their chest again.
  • Taming for the Beastmaster: cave creatures tame with 3 food and fight beside you (they stay with you between visits); bees tame with a touch, circle you and each stings one enemy (10 damage and poison for 6 seconds) before dying, up to 8 at a time.
  • Release or slaughter: the Army window has Release and Slaughter for every tamed animal and creature (Slaughter needs a second tap), and Release all for bees.
  • Castle notice board: after the Quartermaster's fix-up the castle had no notice board or chest, so the recruiter would not work. Nothing is set out until the repairs end, and a missing piece of the castle's or a keep's kit is put back within seconds.
  • Gem walls: every gem leaves a dark wall in its own colour when mined; old blue crystal walls behind standing rock in older worlds are repainted once.
  • Floor pieces fall: mine the block under a chest, workbench, furnace, table, bed or other floor piece and it breaks into your bag; a chest's contents spill on the ground.
  • Miners come back: every 30 seconds away, a miner returns to the foreman's hut with what he dug and starts a fresh trip.
  • Tap a ready checklist: a gold checklist does its job when tapped (place the building, grow the village at your castle, upgrade the shop), handing over a villager's favor first; one not ready says what is missing.
  • Animals step out of blocks: an animal inside solid blocks (under a building you placed, walled in underground) moves to the nearest open spot within a second.
  • Placing preview: a faint picture of the block, back wall, plant, furniture or door in your hand shows where it will go (red and paler where it cannot). The yellow bar for swapping a block now shows on the block (it was drawn off to the side).
  • Woodcutters: each woodcutter puts 6 logs in the lodge chest every morning, cut or not, and they search your whole home island for trees.
  • Goblins stay: an enemy faction taking the goblin forest no longer clears the goblin camps (only you conquering the island does), sleeping no longer removes camp monsters and bosses, and the Goblin Beastmaster returns to his camp three days after he falls. Worlds where a faction had emptied the camps get them back.
  • Every door places: stone, iron and wooden doors go down with you or your soldiers in the doorway, under grass or snow, or aimed at the top half; aim one at an old door to swap them. A message says why when one cannot go down, and a door you choose keeps its kind when the building is rebuilt in stone or iron.
  • Posts on request: walking into a tower or keep no longer opens the soldier posts window. Press F inside and pick Place soldiers here.
  • Tower hatches: the Small tower roof and the Stone watchtower top have a hatch three platforms wide; towers built before are widened once.
  • Exact aim and Hide: on Exact the pickaxe, axe, hoe and hammer work on the block under the mouse (or your tap), not the first block on the way. Under the aim button, Hide takes your following soldiers and pets off the screen while you build; press Show to see them again.
  • Chest names: type a name in the box at the top of a chest window; the Sort by button now looks like the others.
  • Nearby chests: with the crafting or furnace window open, chests you have opened within 15 blocks count as part of your materials (bag first, then chests). Stack to nearby chests in the bag sends each bag item (not the hotbar) to the chests within 15 blocks that already hold it.
  • Crafting by station: a furnace on its own shows only smelting and cooking; a workbench shows its recipes and the by-hand ones; stand by both for everything in one window. The Craft, ×5 and Max panel stays in view while you scroll.
  • Hooks and roofs: the hook never lifts you through a roof; you climb onto a ledge only from beside it with open space over your head.
  • Hook moves: while the hook pulls or holds you, you can mine, attack, place and use items. Press E again to fire a fresh hook where you aim (aiming at the block you already hold climbs up onto it or lets go, as before); jump lets go.
  • Two-wide pieces stand on something: chests, workbenches, workshops, furnaces and thrones need a full, flat block, a platform or another chest or station under both halves. Over a slope, a stair, a half block or air the placing box is red.
  • Keys: Tab opens the bag and E fires the grappling hook (it was X), right beside the movement keys. Players on the old keys are switched over once; keys you set yourself are kept.
  • Saplings in dirt: a tree sapling is planted only on grass, dirt, farmland, sand or snow. Stone, brick, planks and other hard blocks refuse it (a sapling there never grew). Mushroom spores still go on cave stone, moss and the other cave soils.

The God menu has a World tab: build another prepped farm, ripen crops, skip to the next morning, jump to the test farm, set the weather (natural, clear, rain, thunderstorm, snowstorm) and its strength, and start a flash flood, a lightning strike or dry up rain water right away.

Beta 0.5.0

  • Version: the title and pause screens show the Beta version.
  • Bag and chests: the bag holds the hotbar plus 6 rows of 9 (63 slots); chests hold 9 rows of 9 (81). Drag an item out of the bag window, or onto the Drop box beside Delete, to drop it on the ground. Hover over any slot (hotbar, bag, chest) to see the item.
  • Gear locker: a permanent section in the bag keeps tools, weapons and upgrades without using bag space; move them between the locker and the bag at any time. The bag no longer lists "new items"; your upgrades always show and only change when an item is dropped, destroyed or put in a chest.
  • Crafting and shops: Craft All makes as many as you can afford; hold a Craft, Buy or Sell button to repeat it. Every villager buys the goods of their trade (the herbalist flowers, seeds and crops; the blacksmith ores, stones, bars and gems, and so on).
  • Placing stations: furnaces, workbenches, chests and other big stations show their full footprint before you place them, like the building menu.
  • Chests underwater: chests can sit underwater and water flows around them. Chests and furnaces in older buildings were rebuilt to the right size.
  • Torches: a torch can replace grass, flowers and crops (the plant drops its seeds or item; trees are not replaced). Held or Shift torches give no light underwater.
  • Taming whistle: now a permanent upgrade shown with your gear (80 coins at the beast trainer, or crafted). Any class can tame with it by feeding 1 food to a small animal, 2 to a medium one and 3 to a big one. Beastmasters tame without any food.
  • Farms and pens: 4 or more fences in a row on the ground form a pen. Give a tamed animal the Farm job in the Army panel and it settles into the nearest pen; walking into a pen with animals following you asks whether they should stay. Penned animals produce goods every morning and cannot be hurt. Fences are drawn in front of you and your animals.
  • Combat: enemy fighters flank you, archers pick off the weakest target, wounded commanders call their soldiers, and every boss enrages at half health and calls for help (Grukk also slams the ground). Orc crossbowmen now carry crossbows, orc cutthroats daggers and orc brutes warhammers. The ogre hit flash is gone.
  • Factions: enemy factions only build towers on open ground at least 10 blocks from any building made with the world. Their towers have two windows on every floor. Clearing the guards of an enemy outpost or keep captures it. Conquering a castle frees its prisoner automatically.
  • Villager tasks: your shopkeepers offer delivery tasks for coins (up to 3 at a time).
  • Ores: mythril only forms in the sky realm's clouds after the Sky Ranger falls; adamantite only in the magma realm after both realm bosses fall. Chests no longer hold mythril or adamantite bars.
  • Biomes: snow islands have no sand, and deserts never overlap ice, forest or orc islands.
  • Respawning: you and every soldier and pet who respawns with you come back with full health and a full hunger bar.
  • Boats: riders sit and row instead of running.
  • Menus: villager and chest windows close when you walk more than 10 blocks away.
  • Fixes: islands no longer turn black after saving and loading, night monsters spawn again after a reload, and the game no longer freezes when soldiers on a raft fight sea creatures.
  • Ending: slaying Vorthax plays an epilogue and credits; then keep playing, or start New Game Plus with your character in a new world where enemies are 50% tougher for each New Game Plus.
  • Multiplayer: friends who join get the host's real world (castles, doors and buildings as the host sees them), see and use portals, see each other's swings, rowing and other motions, share chest contents, trade with anyone within 5 blocks (T), come back where they left off, and can jump to a friend by tapping them on the map or tapping the arrow that points at them.
