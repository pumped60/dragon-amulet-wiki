Horses are peaceful and run from you. They live in: Grassland, forests, mountains, the desert and ice islands by day: ten kinds, each in its own place.

The ten kinds of horse

A horse is the kind of the place it was born in. A tamed horse keeps its kind, and a foal takes the kind of one of its parents.

Taming

It is tamed with fruit or vegetables: it takes 2, from your bag, with the taming whistle. Beastmasters tame with a touch and need no food. A tamed animal can be a pet (it fights enemies close by and cannot be hurt), a fighter (enemies attack it and it can die), or a farm animal living in a pen; birds can also be gatherers. Tamed animals get their health back a few seconds after a fight ends.
