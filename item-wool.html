Wool is a crafting material. Shorn from sheep. Makes beds, rugs, paintings, grappling hooks and windstep boots.

How to get it

  • Craft: Cloud wool ×2 → Wool ×2 at Workbench
  • Sheep kept in a pen give it each morning
