The Magma Knight

The Magma Knight
Order5 of 8
Health6,500
DamageHeavy, sets you on fire
Soldiers needed8
WhereThrone room of the magma castle (20-30 guards)

The Magma Knight is boss 5 on the ladder.

How it fights

1.5x size, double reach, one great swing at a time; charges, leaps and shockwaves; molten armor (glows for 6 s, takes a fifth of the damage and burns melee attackers until struck by 4 different fighters, then cracks and takes 50% more); lines of erupting fire (watch for the glowing cracks)

Reward

Magma blade, two top permanent upgrades; with the Sky Ranger also beaten, adamantite forms in the magma realm

Bring an army

With fewer soldiers or fighting pets near it than it needs, only a small part of your damage gets through (15% alone) and it heals as the fight goes on. Every boss enrages at half health.
