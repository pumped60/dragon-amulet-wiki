Enemy colonies

Every enemy village is a working colony. Near you, it runs for real: you can watch, raid or stop it. Far from every player it keeps going in the background.

WorkerWhat they do
WoodcutterWalks to a tree near the village, chops for about 8 seconds, fells the whole tree, plants a sapling and carries the wood home.
QuarrymanDigs a pit 4 blocks deep that widens sideways, then carries the stone and any ore home.
FarmerHarvests ripe crops in the village fields, replants them and carries the food home.
BuilderBuilds the next project block by block: castle expansions, watchtowers, houses (150 gold), farms (120 gold) and palisade walls (220 gold, from castle level 2).

Each new house (up to 4) adds a villager, room for one more worker and 3 more soldiers. Killing workers on the road loses the faction what they carry; killing builders stops construction.
