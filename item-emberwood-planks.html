Emberwood planks
TypeBlock
Sells for1 coins
Hardness0.5

Emberwood planks is a block you can place. Dark red planks from the magma realm. They never burn.

How to get it

  • Craft: Emberwood log → Emberwood planks ×4 at By hand
  • Buy from the Shipwright: 90 coins for 20
