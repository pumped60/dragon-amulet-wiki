Ruby is a crafting material. A red gem from ruby ore, imps and golems. Used in fire weapons, the fire rune, staffs and amulets.

How to get it

  • Buy from the Jeweler: 60 coins
  • Dropped by the Goblin beastmaster: 1–2 (60% chance)
  • Dropped by the Imp: 1 (10% chance)
  • Dropped by Grukk the Ogre King: 2–4 (100% chance)
  • Dropped by the Crystal golem: 1 (30% chance)
  • Mined from Ruby ore (with a Gold pickaxe or better), found deep underground, from about halfway down toward the magma realm, and in gem caverns
