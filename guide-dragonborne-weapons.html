Dragonborne weapons

Defeating Vorthax drops the dragonborne weapon of your class; a survival win also unlocks it on this device for good. Only the Dragonborn can start a new world with the dragonborne weapons; beat Vorthax with each of the five classes to unlock all of them. Every hit from one sets the enemy burning.
