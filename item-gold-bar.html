Gold bar is a crafting material. Smelted from 3 gold ore at a furnace. Makes gold tools, weapons and armor, and larger boats.

How to get it

  • Craft: Gold ore ×3 → Gold bar at Furnace
  • Dropped by the Goblin Beastmaster: 2–4 (100% chance)
  • Dropped by the Ogre: 1–2 (35% chance)
  • Dropped by Grukk the Ogre King: 4–8 (100% chance)
  • Dropped by the Mummy: 1 (6% chance)
  • Dropped by the scarab: 1 (3% chance)
  • Mined from Dragon gold
