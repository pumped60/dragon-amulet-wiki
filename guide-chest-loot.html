Chest loot

ChestWhereTypical contents
Wooden chestCaves (shallow to mid), huts, your villageTorches, apples, ore, potions; sometimes a lantern, diving helmet or mana ring. 3 to 30 coins.
Gold chestDeep caves, the dragon lair, realm castles, the orc caveGold, silver and iron bars, diamonds, rubies, sapphires, emeralds; often a cloud charm, sun amulet or arcane ring. About 40 extra coins.
Sky chestSky temples, sky and magma castles, the dragon lairEverything above plus diamonds, life and mana crystals, swiftness and sprint elixirs and a rare accessory (feather charm, sky wings, mermaid charm). About 80 extra coins.

No chest holds mythril or adamantite bars: those come only from the realm ores.
