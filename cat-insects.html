Insects

21 pages. Insects live around you: butterflies and bees by flowers, dragonflies over water, fireflies over grass at night, crickets in tall grass, beetles in woods and caves, sand fleas on beaches and glow flies by glowing mushrooms underground; each cave biome has its own insect, and the sky and magma realms have three each. Catch them alive with a bug net (crafted at a workbench from 3 sticks and 2 wool, or bought from the Fisher); any other tool or weapon, and any arrow or spell, kills them and leaves nothing. Every insect is fishing bait: the catch is rolled twice and the better taken (three times with a realm insect), and the insect is kept after a catch 35% of the time (70% on the antler rod). They sell to traders.
